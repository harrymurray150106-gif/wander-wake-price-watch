Recent price changes

Prices are pulled automatically from each store's own public product data on a schedule (see .github/workflows/update-prices.yml). Retailers that block automated checks (Walmart, Newegg) are shown with a verified by hand label and the date the price was checked. A source that failed its last automated check is flagged stale and keeps its last confirmed price — Price Watch never invents a price. Every competitor listing is matched to the same manufacturer model number as the Wander & Wake product; click a retailer name to open its listing.
