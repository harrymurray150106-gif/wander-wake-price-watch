Prices are pulled directly from each store's own public product data on a schedule (see .github/workflows/update-prices.yml), not estimated or manually entered. A store not updating here usually means its last scheduled check failed (flagged as stale) rather than a real price of zero — Price Watch never invents a price. See this repository's README for how prices are checked and how to add more products.
